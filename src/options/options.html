Welcome! Three steps to get going

  1. Connect an AI model below with your own API key. It powers live insights, answers and summaries.
  2. Pick how meetings are captured: audio transcription (any site, best quality) or the meeting's own live captions (free, no extra key).
  3. Join a meeting, click the Smart Meet toolbar icon and press Start. Tip: pin the extension from the puzzle-piece menu.

Privacy & data

  • Transcripts, insights and chats are stored only in this browser (IndexedDB). There is no Smart Meet server.
  • Audio and text are sent directly from your browser to the AI and transcription providers you configure, under your own API keys and their terms.
  • API keys are stored in this browser's extension storage and are never synced or sent anywhere else.
