# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-02

First release.

- Live detection of questions, clarification points, action items, decisions and key points, with suggested replies and streamed "Draft answer".
- Audio capture (meeting tab and microphone) with pause-aware chunking, or live-caption reading on Google Meet, Zoom web and Teams web.
- Ask AI over any meeting (live or past), with timestamp citations and retrieval for very long meetings.
- Automatic end-of-meeting summary, a searchable history, and Markdown/JSON export.
- LLM providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama, and custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Speech-to-text: OpenAI, Groq, Deepgram, and custom Whisper servers.
- Local-only storage, optional automatic deletion, and a consent prompt before the first capture.
